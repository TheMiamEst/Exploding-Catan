PROFILE PICTURES
================

Drop portraits in here as 1.png, 2.png, 3.png … up to 40.png, then reload the
game. They show up in the picture picker: New Game, Online before you host or
join, or by clicking your own portrait on your seat card during a game.

  PNG, square, 256x256

They are shown in a CIRCLE about 64px across, so keep the subject central and
expect the corners to be cut off. Faces read better than scenes at that size.
No transparency needed.

Numbered rather than named because the game probes for them: it asks for each
in turn and keeps whatever answers. So adding one is dropping a file in — no
list to edit anywhere, and gaps are fine: a missing number just means nobody
drew that one. 404s in the browser console for the numbers you have not used
are expected and harmless.

A folder of its own so that actual pictures of people stay separate from hex
tiles and card faces, and the ONLY place the game looks for a portrait. It
used to fall back to offering the card faces and tiles in ../ while this
folder was empty; it does not any more. An empty folder means everybody plays
as their initial on their seat colour, which is a perfectly good answer.

Only the picture's NUMBER travels between browsers, never the file. Everybody
does not need the same profile folder: somebody playing with a picture you have
not got shows up on your screen as their initial on their seat colour, which is
also what everyone gets before they pick anything.
